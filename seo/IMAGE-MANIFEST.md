### Hero Decision

gbp-02 (red semi on snowy highway shoulder) is the strongest hero candidate because:
- Portrait orientation works for mobile-first hero
- Snowy sky area provides clean text overlay zone at top
- Shows actual roadside work — the business's key differentiator
- Atmospheric winter setting reinforces "we're there when you need us"
- Dark scrim at 0.45 opacity over the full image keeps Bone (#F5F0E8) text at AA contrast against the mixed sky/snow background
